Entry for the [The 3AM Fridge Text](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deysumbrella-the-3am-fridge-text-4c3e721d) bounty on make.fun: the one line a smart fridge would text its owner at 3am, up to 140 characters, judged head to head by an AI judge.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (133 characters, no trailing newline) and verifies byte-for-byte that the file matches the line.

> It's 3am. You opened me, stared into the cold for eleven seconds, and closed me. I'm not a mirror. I'm a fridge. Talk to a therapist.
